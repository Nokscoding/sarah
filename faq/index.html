Get in Touch
Do I need an appointment?+

Yes. Sarah Stitches works by appointment only. Please request a consultation online or call in advance.

Can I get a quote online?+

Share the garment type, photographs if available and what you would like changed. Final pricing depends on the individual request.

Is an online booking instantly confirmed?+

No. The form submits a request; an appointment is confirmed only after Sarah Stitches follows up.

How quickly can my garment be finished?+

Turnaround depends on the garment, the requested work and current availability. Please mention your needed date when contacting us.

What payment methods do you accept?+

Cash, Cash App and Zelle. Confirm payment details directly with the business before making a payment.

Do you offer custom sewing?+

Yes. Use the service request form to describe your design ideas and ask whether your project is possible.

Can I request dress alterations for a special occasion?+

Yes, describe your dress, the fit you need and your event date. Suitability and timing depend on the garment. Read about dress alterations.

Can you shorten pants or hem jeans?+

You can request a pants hemming consultation. Fabric and original construction affect the best finish. Learn about pants hemming.

What if a zipper on my garment is broken?+

Send details of the garment and zipper issue to discuss repair or replacement options. Explore zipper repair.

Can a dress be taken in at the waist?+

Some dresses allow changes to waist or bodice fit, depending on the fabric, seams and lining. A consultation helps assess the options.
