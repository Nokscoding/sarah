FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before your appointment.

What should I bring to a dress fitting?+

Bring the dress and, if relevant, the shoes and undergarments you plan to wear.

Can every dress be altered?+

No. Seam allowance, lining, fabric and construction determine what is possible.

How much does dress hemming cost?+

Pricing depends on the individual garment and requested work. Request a consultation.
