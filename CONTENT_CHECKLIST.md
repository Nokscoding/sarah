# Before final public launch

- Verify logo and all contact details with the client.
- Replace editorial inspiration photos with approved project photographs from the full local asset pack.
- Set the final domain in canonical, Open Graph, sitemap and robots files.
- Add the verified city and service area for local SEO; do not invent an address.
- Enable Netlify Forms, set email notification, and test a genuine submission.
- Have client approve services, payment methods and privacy policy.
- Connect Google Search Console and submit sitemap.
